# The 30-entity sample

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 02

## Question

Which 30 entities from the Legendary level make up the first batch? Pick 3–4 connected clusters across People, Conflicts and Milestones (e.g. Drake, Elizabeth I, the Armada, Philip II) so Connection hopping can be judged, and check that each cluster's likely targets exist at Legendary.

## Answer

Thirty entities, all at Legendary, in five clusters (Legendary holds 76 People, 9 Conflicts and 38 Milestones; Drake, Philip II and the Armada are not among them):

- WWII / Cold War: Adolf Hitler, Joseph Stalin, Albert Einstein; World War II, World War I, Cold War; The Holocaust, First nuclear weapon, United Nations, NATO.
- Scientific revolution: Nicolaus Copernicus, Galileo Galilei, Isaac Newton, Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz, René Descartes, Blaise Pascal; Gregorian calendar.
- French Revolution: Napoleon, Louis XIV of France, Jean-Jacques Rousseau, Thomas Jefferson; French Revolution.
- Rome: Julius Caesar, Augustus, Cleopatra; Founding of the Roman Empire.
- Tudors and Reformation: Elizabeth I of England, Henry VIII of England, Martin Luther, William Shakespeare.

That is 20 People, 4 Conflicts, 6 Milestones. One prompt serves every lane, so all thirty run in one batch.
