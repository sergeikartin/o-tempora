# Formalize Milestone Category Group; Conflicts filtered as a single toggle, not per-category

Adding sidebar filters for Conflicts and Milestones surfaced that only Milestones has ever had a color-driving grouping above its category values (3 groups over 20 leaf `MilestoneCategory` values, previously just unnamed local color constants in `options.ts`); Conflicts' per-category color palette was retired earlier in favor of one flat `CONFLICT_COLOR`, so no grouping exists there. We formalized the Milestone grouping as a shared `MilestoneCategoryGroup` type + `MILESTONE_CATEGORY_TO_GROUP` map in `packages/shared-types` (so color logic and the new filter read one canonical source instead of duplicating it), and filter Milestones by those 3 groups — the literal match for "same as color grouping." For Conflicts, rather than inventing a group layer with no color meaning, or filtering by 6 ungrouped raw categories, we ship a single blanket "Conflicts" visibility toggle: the two lanes' filters are intentionally asymmetric because their underlying category structures already are.

**Considered for Conflicts:** (a) 6 individual raw-category pills — rejected as granularity with no payoff, since there's no color to key the distinction off; (b) a new group layer invented purely for filtering — rejected as added complexity with no reuse elsewhere in the UI.

**Addendum (2026-08-12, later the same day):** the granularity decision above (Conflicts stays all-or-nothing, never per-category) is unchanged, but the *delivery mechanism* was revised twice after initial implementation. First to a differently-styled, inverted-default toggle switch, then — per feedback that the whole "Conflicts & Milestones" section should behave as one multi-select filter, the same shape as Region/Occupation Domain — folded into the same flat pill list as the 3 Milestone Category Group pills, via a UI-only `'conflicts'` sentinel value (`ConflictsMilestonesFilterValue` in `packages/web/src/shared/config/conflicts-milestones-filter.ts`). There is now exactly one control, one selection array, and one "empty means unfiltered" convention for this entire sidebar section — not two differently-shaped controls that happen to render in the same box. See `.scratch/conflicts-milestones-category-filters/spec.md`'s amendments for the full history.

**Addendum (2026-08-13):** the 3-group split (`knowledge-culture` / `technology-industry` / `society-governance`) was replaced with a 2-group split, `science-innovation` / `social-culture` (display labels "Science & Innovation" / "Social & Human Culture"). Motivation was a curation session checking whether that coarser Science-vs-Social framing was evenly distributed across the ~230 milestones — it wasn't at first (65/35), narrowed through a series of per-item recategorizations (moving mis-filed items like railway infrastructure out of `transportation`, dropping low-signal computing noise from `everyday-technology`) down to a ~58/42 split, still short of even. Closing the rest of the gap required moving whole categories across the Science/Social line (`environment-geology` into Social) and, for `medicine-health`, splitting it — its pandemic/public-health-administration items don't share medicine-health's "primarily a treatment/technology" character. Since `MILESTONE_CATEGORY_TO_GROUP` maps whole leaf categories to a group (no per-item override — matches the pipeline's own "no manual override, fix the tag logic and re-run" convention, see `packages/data-pipeline/CLAUDE.md`), the split required a new leaf category, `public-health`, carved out of `medicine-health`. The 3-pill filter above is now a 2-pill filter; group swatch colors are unchanged (`#008456`/`#BC8118`, dropping `#4B4597`) — see `packages/web/docs/design-tokens.md`'s Milestone Category Palette for the current mapping and CVD validation.

**Addendum (2026-08-13, later the same day):** a further review surfaced two more issues with the fresh Science-vs-Social split. First, 8 more individually-miscategorized items (Great Irish Famine, Penny Black, BBC Television Service launch, Terracotta Army, Lucy (Australopithecus), Facebook, Workers Leaving the Lumière Factory — Green Revolution was considered but left in Science on reflection, closer in kind to an industrial-technology milestone than a social one). Second, and found independently while spot-checking the rendered timeline: Eiffel Tower (`architecture-design` → Social) and Panama Canal (`infrastructure` → Science) rendered in different colors despite both reading as monumental engineering/construction landmarks — an artifact of how the ad hoc Science/Social split happened to land, not a deliberate call (the original 3-group taxonomy had both categories together under "Technology & Industry"). Resolved by: moving `archaeology-anthropology` wholesale to `social-culture` (3 items, small and cohesive); folding Great Irish Famine into the existing `public-health` category (thematically a mass-mortality disaster, same as Black Death/1918 flu/COVID-19); splitting a new `media-culture` leaf category out of `communication` for the 4 media/social items, leaving `communication`'s hard-tech inventions (Telephone, Internet, GSM, etc.) in Science; and retiring `architecture-design`, merging it with `infrastructure`'s single-structure items (bridges, canals, dams, tunnels) into a new `landmarks` category → Social, while `infrastructure` keeps only its transit-network items (railways, highway system, subway) → Science. `MILESTONE_CATEGORIES` grew from 21 to 22 leaf values net (`media-culture` and `landmarks` added, `architecture-design` removed). No group/color changes — still `science-innovation`/`social-culture`, `#008456`/`#BC8118`.
