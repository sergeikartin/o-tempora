# Profile verification

The [Profile coordinator](profile-coordinator.md#english) runs `english-adversarial`, `english-audit` and `english-repair` for the selected QID. Each command prepares a task or applies a reply bound to that task’s inputs. The auditor receives the complete Profile and assessment from the writer or the current verification report.

## Approval policy

- Extract claims in passage order from every visible field, including overview, dates, titles and Connection explanations. Read each Key Moment's three fields together for implied claims. The writer supplies findings with each passage. An independent auditor checks each passage against the evidence and then checks the full narrative for chronology, contradictions, repetition and balance.
- Supporting excerpts must occur in locally fetched public HTTPS HTML/plain-text sources. Research reads bounded excerpts by phrase or offset. The audit receives the Profile once and each finding once, with short numeric references to shared source excerpts. New HTML snapshots record a title when available and heading offsets. The auditor sees each quote in its enclosing section, and snapshots saved without headings remain readable. Quote and anchor checks use the full cached sources. Reports retain excerpts, URLs and content hashes, and full snapshots live in gitignored `data/raw/profiles/evidence/`. PDFs and login-only pages require a readable alternative.
- A finding's optional `counterevidence` carries exact excerpts from credible fetched accounts that materially conflict with or limit the claim or its supporting account. The audit checks those excerpts in context and verifies that any attribution or qualification resolves the conflict. Only supporting evidence can establish corroboration or support a displayed passage citation.
- Adversarial verification checks up to five supported findings before the English audit, prioritizing Deep Facts, corroboration risks, and cues for long quotations, superlatives or legends. It looks for limiting or contradictory accounts with neutral and falsification queries. Fetched counterevidence joins the assessment, and the auditor decides whether a conflict requires a change. Policy 7 reports require a check for every selected finding and continue to accept historical receipts that recorded an explicit opt-out.
- Every remaining claim must be `supported`. `contradicted`, `unverifiable`, `disputed`, uncovered details and failed mechanical checks block approval. Obscure anecdotes, quotations, firsts/superlatives, disputed figures and living-person allegations require two independent original accounts on distinct hosts, including primary or scholarly support. Confidence never grants approval. Clearly attributed legends verify the existence of the account.
- Mechanical checks cover schema/markup, subject and Connection QIDs, reversed year labels, historical year zero, and verifier-extracted birth/death or event start/end years against structured dataset dates. Ongoing subjects have a known start and an open end. Approximate dataset dates allow one year of tolerance. Other dates and calendar distinctions are evidence/audit checks. Public passage citations must point to fetched supporting pages, with valid section anchors.
- A receipt binds the policy, content, identity, locale and report hashes. Output withholds absent, malformed, stale or failing approvals. Changed dataset dates or edited content require re-verification before publishing. The `edited` status protects prose and still requires approval. Current and version 3 through 6 approvals remain eligible under the same checks. Policy 3 added a start-year check for ongoing subjects, so their version 2 reports require re-verification. Version 2 reports for closed spans and point dates are still eligible. Bump `POLICY_VERSION` whenever approval rules or verifier instructions change. ## Repair and review

The coordinator allows up to two draft changes. Correct or qualify prose from evidence and remove unsupported optional Deep Facts or Connections as needed. Preserve identity and substantive core history. Prior drafts and reports live under `verification-history/`. Protected `edited` records receive a candidate in `proposals/`. A human can apply the proposal and submit it for verification.

Adversarial checks can be reused from the current or archived reports when their passage path and claim text match and any counterevidence still passes snapshot validation. Send the remaining findings to the adversarial checker and submit the full Profile to a fresh auditor after a repair. Evidence-only repairs preserve the remaining draft repair rounds.

Each `<profile>.verification.json` records the claims, evidence, corrections, blockers and model IDs, along with any adversarial queries, outcomes and notes. Audit outcomes are `approved`, `skipped` and `held`. A held result exits nonzero. Retry failed source reads or seek a readable source before changing prose.

Humans handle essential factual/framing disagreements and protected-edit proposals. Initially audit a small selection of approvals across all three lanes, including repaired Profiles and corroborated claims; expand audits when a failure appears. Automatic approval is fallible, so use audit failures to improve fixtures and policy. Held Profiles keep the existing Description fallback.
