# Profile verification

The [Profile coordinator](profile-coordinator.md#english) runs `english-audit` and `english-repair` for the selected QID. Each command prepares a task or applies a reply bound to that task’s inputs. Use `--out=<file>` to save a prepared task in gitignored `data/raw/profiles/tasks/<QID>/` for a fresh subagent or a cross-family audit. Apply replies with `--result=<file>`. The auditor receives the complete Profile and assessment from `tasks/<QID>/pending.json` or the current verification report. [Cross-family handoff](profile-handoff.md)

## Approval policy

- Extract claims in passage order from every visible field, including overview, dates, titles and Connection explanations. Read each Key Moment's three fields together for implied claims. The writer supplies findings with each passage. An independent auditor checks each passage against the evidence and then checks the full narrative for chronology, contradictions, repetition and balance.
- Supporting excerpts must occur in locally fetched public HTTPS HTML/plain-text sources. Research reads bounded excerpts by phrase or offset. The audit receives the Profile once and each finding once, with short numeric references to shared source excerpts. New HTML snapshots record a title when available and heading offsets. The auditor sees each quote in its enclosing section, and snapshots saved without headings remain readable. Quote and anchor checks use the full cached sources. Reports retain excerpts, URLs and content hashes, and full snapshots live in gitignored `data/raw/profiles/evidence/`. PDFs and login-only pages require a readable alternative.
- Counterevidence discovery runs upfront during scout/prefetch and drafting: `profile-scout` pairs Deep Fact candidates with `dispute_urls`, `prefetch` caches them as `dispute_source_ids`, and the writer records material competing or limiting accounts in `finding.counterevidence`. The auditor checks those excerpts in context and verifies that any attribution or qualification resolves the conflict. Only supporting evidence can establish corroboration or support a displayed passage citation.
- Every remaining claim must be `supported`. `contradicted`, `unverifiable`, `disputed`, uncovered details and failed mechanical checks block approval. Obscure anecdotes, quotations, firsts/superlatives, disputed figures and living-person allegations require two independent original accounts on distinct hosts, including primary or scholarly support. Confidence never grants approval. Clearly attributed legends verify the existence of the account.
- Mechanical checks cover schema/markup, subject and Connection QIDs, reversed year labels, historical year zero, and verifier-extracted birth/death or event start/end years against structured dataset dates. Ongoing subjects have a known start and an open end. Approximate dataset dates allow one year of tolerance. Other dates and calendar distinctions are evidence/audit checks. Public passage citations must point to fetched supporting pages, with valid section anchors.
- A receipt binds the policy version, content, identity, locale and report hashes. At audit time, the verifier always enforces the current rules (`POLICY_VERSION`) and stamps that version as provenance metadata. At publication time, Output verifies the receipt hashes, dataset `qid` and dates, review status (`reviewed` or `edited`), and empty stored blockers/errors without re-running current target-selection rules against previously approved reports. Changed dataset dates or edited content require re-verification before publishing. The `edited` status protects prose from overwrite by `finalize` or `english-repair` and still requires approval. ## Repair and review

The coordinator allows up to two draft changes, tracked alongside pending assessments in gitignored `tasks/<QID>/pending.json`. `english-repair` prepares a compact task with only the Profile, blockers, and flagged findings, and accepts a compact JSON patch containing only changed passages (merging untouched passages and findings verbatim; full assessed Profile replies remain accepted). Correct or qualify prose from evidence and remove unsupported optional Deep Facts or Connections as needed. Preserve identity and substantive core history. Protected `edited` records cannot have their prose modified by automatic repair (evidence-only updates remain allowed, and human edits can be re-audited directly).

Submit the repaired Profile to a fresh auditor with `re_audit` metadata (`repaired_paths` and `prior_blockers`) so re-audits verify resolved blockers and patched passages without reopening untouched wording on minor nitpicks. Evidence-only repairs preserve the remaining draft repair rounds.

Each `<profile>.verification.json` records the claims, evidence, corrections, blockers and model IDs. Set the serving model ID in every reply’s top-level `model`. The report’s `models` array records the assessment and audit models. Audit outcomes are `approved`, `skipped` and `held`. A held result exits nonzero. Retry failed source reads or seek a readable source before changing prose.

Humans handle essential factual/framing disagreements and protected `edited` prose updates. Initially audit a small selection of approvals across all three lanes, including repaired Profiles and corroborated claims; expand audits when a failure appears. Automatic approval is fallible, so use audit failures to improve fixtures and policy. Held Profiles keep the existing Description fallback.
