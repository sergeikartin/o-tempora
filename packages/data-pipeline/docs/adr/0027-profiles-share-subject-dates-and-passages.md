# Profile verification and Output share subject dates and passage paths

Derive Wikidata identity and structured astronomical years from transformed rows in one module for Profile subjects and Output targets. Verification compares the known years directly, including the start year of an ongoing subject. Render the established date labels for prompts, reports and freshness checks so existing receipts retain their date identity.

Issue new reports under verification policy 3. Version 2 reports for closed spans and point dates remain eligible because they passed the same year comparisons. Version 2 reports for ongoing subjects require re-verification because the old label parser skipped their start-year comparison.

Use one traversal of the established passage paths for evidence coverage, translation and Output. Keep each reader-facing field tied to its existing path, and preserve the English Profile’s structure, citations, legend flags and Connection identities when replacing prose.
